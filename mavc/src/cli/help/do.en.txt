Add audio files:
  -+ is the short command for adding songs; it is equivalent to --add and add.
  mavc do -+ song.mp3 other.flac --to collection.mavc
  mavc do add song.mp3 other.flac --to a.mavc b.mavc
  Supported audio: MP3, FLAC, AAC (ADTS), WAV (PCM / IEEE float), OGG (Vorbis). Imports are checked before writing.

Remove tracks (IDs, not audio paths):
  mavc list collection.mavc
  mavc do -x 0 2 --from collection.mavc

Export before removal (optional):
  mavc do --remove 0 2 --from collection.mavc --to ./export
  mavc do -x 0 --from collection.mavc --to ./export ./backup

Rules:
  -+ / --add / add adds songs; -x / --remove / remove removes track IDs.
  --from accepts multiple archives. --to accepts multiple destinations and can be repeated.
  Without --to, removal deletes tracks directly. With --to, songs are exported under their original filenames.
  Export directories are created automatically. All exports must succeed before removal starts.
  Existing files, duplicate output names, or export failures stop removal and preserve archive tracks.
  No configured track-count or audio-payload limit.

Detailed options:
  mavc do --add --help
  mavc do --remove --help
